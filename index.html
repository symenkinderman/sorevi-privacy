Before you start

Is Sorevi free?

There is free access with limits on AI usage. Optional Premium gives higher AI usage limits and access to additional practice scenarios and challenges. Prices are shown in the app before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your app-store account.

Is it available on iPhone or iPad?

Sorevi is currently available to download on Google Play for Android. The iOS version is not yet publicly available on the App Store.

Do I have to use voice?

No. You can practise with text. Voice practice is optional and requires microphone permission.

Who processes my AI conversations?

AI features use OpenAI through Sorevi’s backend. The app explains which data is sent and asks for your permission before sharing it. Avoid entering sensitive information. Read the full policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Account deletion and data export are available in Settings. See account deletion information. Deleting your account does not cancel an existing store subscription.
