Sorevi Support

Help with Sorevi accounts, subscriptions, app features, and technical issues.

Contact support

If you need help with Sorevi, email sorevi.support@gmail.com.

When reporting a technical issue, please describe what happened and which device/platform you are using. Do not send your password or other sensitive authentication information.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. You can manage or cancel a subscription through your app-store account settings. If an existing purchase is not recognized in Sorevi, use the Restore Purchases option in the app.

Account access and deletion

Sorevi includes account controls in the app. If you need information about deleting your account, see the Sorevi account deletion page.

Privacy

For information about how Sorevi processes information, see the Sorevi Privacy Policy.
